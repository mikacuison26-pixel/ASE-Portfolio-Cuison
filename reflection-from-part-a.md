After finishing the Concept Analysis, I understood Agentic Software Engineering better. Saw how it is different from normal software development. I learned that AI agents can do more than just follow orders, it can read data, decide what to do and act to reach goals. I also saw that building these systems needs planning, teamwork and constant improvement. Agile methods help developers change their work and fix problems as they come. Tools such as GitHub help organize files. Keep a record of changes.
Most important, I learned that creating AI-powered applications requires responsibility. Developers must think about mistakes, security threats, data privacy and unfair choices. Human oversight is needed so that AI systems are used right and do not cause harm. Overall, this activity taught me that building AI systems is not only about what they can do but also about making sure they are safe, reliable and responsible.
